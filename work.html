Case Studies

Suburban Family Home Transformation

Challenge: A 1970s family home needed modernization while maintaining comfort and functionality for a family of four.

Solution: We implemented an open floor plan, updated the color palette to neutral tones, added modern lighting fixtures, and selected contemporary furniture that maximized space.

Result: The homeowners gained an additional 200 square feet of perceived space and increased property value by 15%.

Luxury Penthouse Interior Design

Challenge: A high-end penthouse required an upscale design that reflected the owner's sophisticated aesthetic and entertained frequently.

Solution: We designed a luxury interior featuring custom furnishings, premium materials, statement lighting, and an open living concept perfect for entertaining.

Result: Successfully created a magazine-worthy space that became a showcase property and won several interior design awards.

Eco-Conscious Apartment Redesign

Challenge: An environmentally conscious client wanted sustainable design without compromising on style or comfort.

Solution: We integrated eco-friendly materials, energy-efficient lighting, sustainable wood furniture, and plants for improved air quality.

Result: Reduced the client's energy consumption by 30% while creating a beautiful, healthy living environment.
